How to use Tube Checker

Tube Checker turns a list of pathology tests into the blood tubes you need, in the order of draw, following NSW Health Pathology and RCPA conventions. It runs on your device, works offline, and stores no patient data.

Add your tests

  • Search and tap tests, with typo tolerance and common abbreviations (FBC, UEC, LFT).
  • Or paste a whole list at once and it splits them out.
  • Each test becomes a chip: tap to edit, × to remove, + to add another.

Scan or upload a form

  • Tap Scan with camera to photograph a request form; it reads the tests automatically.
  • For more than one page, tap the + tile next to the photo to add another.
  • Or upload existing photos or scans instead.

Read the tubes

  • Tubes are listed in the order to draw them, with the cap colour and a number.
  • Quantities account for large panels and send-away tests.
  • An approximate total volume is shown for syringe draws.

Send-away tests

Some tests are not run at every site and are sent to a reference lab. A test that may need referral shows an Off site? tag; tap it to switch to Send separately and the list adds a separate tube. Always confirm with your local lab.

Test references

Test names follow the RCPA Manual, which is the primary reference. At the bottom of the page, each requested test links to its RCPA entry and its NSW catalogue entry, so you can quickly check the authoritative tube, specimen and handling details. The references shown can vary by your state and region.

Share and install

  • Share summary sends or copies a clean tube list.
  • Add to Home Screen to use it like an app, offline.

Privacy

Everything happens on your device and nothing is sent anywhere. Do not enter or photograph identifiable patient information. Tube Checker is a decision-support aid only, not a substitute for professional judgement or your laboratory's collection manual. See the terms of use.
