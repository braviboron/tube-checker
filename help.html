How to use Tube Checker

Work out the right blood tubes, in the right order, in seconds.

What it does

Tube Checker takes a list of pathology tests, from a request form, and tells you which collection tubes you need, how many, and the order of draw, using NSW Health Pathology and RCPA conventions. It runs entirely on your device, works offline, and stores no patient data.

Quick start

  1. Add your tests — type or search, paste a whole list, or scan/upload a form.
  2. Read the result — the tubes appear in order of draw, with quantities and an approximate total volume.
  3. Share or collect — tap Share summary, or just work down the list.

Adding tests

  • Search and tap tests one at a time, with typo tolerance and common abbreviations (FBC, UEC, LFT...).
  • Paste a list — paste several tests at once and it splits them out.
  • Each detected test becomes an editable chip: tap to edit, × to remove, + to add another.

Scan or upload a form

  • Scan with camera — take a photo of the request form; it reads the text automatically and adds the tests.
  • More than one page? Tap the + tile next to the photo to capture another; each page is read once.
  • Upload existing photos or scans instead, if you prefer.
On an iPhone you can also press and hold the search box, choose AutoFill → Scan Text, and point at the form — the phone's own text recognition is excellent.

Reading the tubes

  • Tubes are listed in the order you should draw them, numbered, with the cap colour.
  • Quantities account for both large panels and send-away tests.
  • An approximate total volume is shown for syringe draws.
Light blue (citrate) Gold (serum) Purple (EDTA) Pink (blood bank)

Send-away (off-site) tests

Some specialised tests are not run at every hospital and are sent to a reference lab. Tube Checker flags these so you do not miss a separate tube.

  • A test that may need referral shows an Off site? tag on its chip.
  • Tap the tag to switch it to Send separately; the tube list updates so that test gets its own tube.
  • Always confirm referral details against your local pathology service.

Share and install

  • Share summary sends a clean tube list via your phone's share sheet, or copies it.
  • Add to Home Screen to use it like an app, full-screen and fully offline.
  • Your site (bottom of the page) lets you save your hospital; this is being built out and will soon tailor results to your site.

Privacy & safety

Everything happens on your device and nothing is sent anywhere. Do not enter or photograph identifiable patient information. Tube Checker is a decision-support aid only, not a substitute for professional judgement or your laboratory's official collection manual — always verify against your local pathology service before collecting blood.
