Decision-support aid only, not a substitute for professional judgement or your laboratory's official collection manual. Tube types, additives and the order of draw vary between laboratories and over time, so always verify against your local pathology service before collecting blood. Use of this tool is subject to its terms of use.

All processing happens on your device and nothing is sent anywhere. However, protecting patient confidentiality is your responsibility: do not enter, photograph or expose identifiable patient information, and follow your workplace's privacy and data-handling policies.

Sources: the RCPA Manual is the primary per-test reference, with test names aligned to it; each requested test links to its RCPA entry (and its NSW Health Pathology Test Catalogue entry for local availability and tube details). Also: NSW Health Pathology (collection guidance); NSW CEC Adult Blood Culture Guidance (v3.0, 2024); RCPA Manual blood tube chart and order of draw.
