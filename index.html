⚠ CLINICAL DISCLAIMER

This tool is a decision-support aid only and is not a substitute for professional judgement or your laboratory's official collection manual. Tube types, additives and the order of draw can vary between laboratories and over time. Always verify requirements against your local pathology service before collecting blood. No patient data is stored or transmitted.
